A Field Guide to AI Agent Jargon

Eight terms you'll hear constantly around AI agents — spotted and labeled, so the next conversation doesn't send you searching.

🔤
specimen 01

Token

A small chunk of text — often a word piece — that the model reads and generates one at a time.

🪟
specimen 02

Context Window

Everything the model can "see" at once — your messages, its replies, and any documents, all at the same time.

✍️
specimen 03

Prompt

The instructions or question you give the model to get a response back.

🎲
specimen 04

Temperature

A setting that controls how random or predictable the model's word choices are — low is safe, high is creative.

👻
specimen 05

Hallucination

When the model confidently states something false as if it were fact — it isn't lying, it's just wrong.

🎯
specimen 06

Fine-tuning

Extra training on a specific dataset to specialize a general model's behavior for one job.

🕶️
specimen 07

System Prompt

Hidden instructions set before the conversation starts, shaping how the model behaves the whole way through.

📎
specimen 08

Grounding

Connecting an answer to real, checkable external data instead of relying only on what the model memorized.

✎ Know these eight, and 90% of AI agent conversations stop feeling like a foreign language.
