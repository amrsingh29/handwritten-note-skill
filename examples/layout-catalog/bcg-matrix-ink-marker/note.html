The BCG Growth-Share Matrix

One simple question decides where a business sits: is the market growing, and are you winning in it? Plot both, and you get four very different plans of action.

Question Marks

Fast-growing market, but you're not winning it yet. Could become a Star with real investment — or quietly fail. High risk, high upside.

⭐

Stars

Growing market and you're leading it. Worth pouring resources into — this is where tomorrow's Cash Cows come from.

🐶

Dogs

Slow market, weak position. Rarely worth further investment — the honest move is usually to divest or wind down.

🐄

Cash Cows

Slow-growing market, but you dominate it. Needs little new investment and throws off steady profit — funds everything else.
