How LFP Batteries Work

A battery isn't a container of energy — it's a shuttle for lithium ions, running the same short trip back and forth, thousands of times over.

🔋
the framework barely wears out — that's the whole trick
plug in

Charging

External power pushes lithium ions out of the iron-phosphate cathode, across the electrolyte, toward the graphite anode.

stored

Ions Settle In

Ions wedge themselves between layers of graphite at the anode — the battery now holds usable energy.

structure holds

Framework Survives

Unlike other lithium chemistries, the iron-phosphate structure barely shifts shape doing this — so it can go again, and again.

powering up

Discharging

Plugged into a device, ions flow back to the cathode — electrons take the long way through the circuit, doing the actual work.

✎ LFP isn't a different way to store energy — it's a chemistry built to survive doing it thousands of times over.
