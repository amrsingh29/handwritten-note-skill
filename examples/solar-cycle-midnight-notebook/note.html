The Sun's 11-Year Cycle

The Sun isn't constant — it swings between calm and violent on a clock, and we're currently living through the rising side of it.

☀︎
~11 years, then the poles flip and it starts again
quiet sun

Solar Minimum

Few sunspots, a calm magnetic field. The sun looks almost featureless.

building up

Rising Phase

The internal magnetic field tangles and strengthens; sunspots start multiplying.

peak activity

Solar Maximum

Sunspots, solar flares, and coronal mass ejections all peak — the sun at its most violent.

winding down

Declining Phase

Activity fades as the tangled field untangles — heading back toward minimum.

✎ Solar Cycle 25 began in 2020 — we're watching this exact loop happen right now.
